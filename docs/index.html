Cristiano Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo is a Portuguese professional footballer who plays as a forward for Saudi Pro League club Al Nassr and the Portugal national team.

Ronaldo is widely regarded as one of the greatest players of all time.

About

Cristiano Ronaldo was born on February 5, 1985, in Funchal, Madeira, Portugal.

He started playing football at a young age and became a professional footballer at Sporting CP.

Ronaldo is known for his speed, technique, goalscoring ability and hard work.

Football Career

Sporting CP

Ronaldo started his professional career at Sporting CP.

Manchester United

He became famous around the world while playing for Manchester United.

Real Madrid

Ronaldo had a very successful career at Real Madrid.

Juventus

Ronaldo played for Juventus in Italy.

Al Nassr

Ronaldo currently plays for Al Nassr.

Achievements

  • Five Ballon d'Or awards
  • Four European Golden Shoes
  • UEFA Champions League winner
  • European Championship winner
  • UEFA Nations League winner
  • Over 800 career goals
